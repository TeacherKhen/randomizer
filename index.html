How to Use

Follow these simple steps to randomly assign students, groups, roles, or classroom tasks.

1
👤 Add your students Go to Settings and type one student's name on each line.
2
🎭 Add your roles or tasks Type one role or task on each line. For example: doctor, teacher, leader, reader.
3
🖼️ Add pictures if you want You can upload a picture for each role or task. This is optional and helps students understand the assignment visually.
4
💾 Save and go to the game Click “SAVE & GO TO GAME” when your students and roles are ready.
5
👤 Choose a mode Choose Singular for individual assignments, Plural / Groups for group assignments, or Mixed for a combination of individual students and groups.
6
🎲 Press RANDOMIZE The game will randomly assign the students and roles. Press it again whenever you want a new random arrangement.
7
🔊 Use the extra tools Use READ to hear the assignments, PRINT to print the results, and HELP if you need a quick reminder.
💡 Teacher Tip: You can randomize again anytime. Each time you press RANDOMIZE, the students and groups can receive a new arrangement.

How to Use the Game

1. Singular: Every student receives an individual role or task.

2. Plural / Groups: Students are randomly divided into groups. Each group receives one role or task.

3. Mixed: Some students receive individual roles, while the remaining students are randomly divided into multiple groups. The number of groups can change each time you randomize.

4. Randomize: Press RANDOMIZE to create a new assignment.

5. Pictures: You can upload a picture for each role or task.

Tip: For singular and plural roles, you can use the format doctor-doctors.
